PROCEDURE FOR APPLICATION FOR COLLEGE ADMISSION
1. Access the admission link on a website browser then sign up with a Gmail account. Download and Fill out the online application form, Application Form keep in mind the following:

* The declared track/strand in the online application form and the track/strand indicated in the documentary requirement should match.

* Choose the correct entry type/category of applicant.

* Thoroughly check all information input in the online application form before saving and submitting to avoid typographical errors, wrong spelling, incorrect entries, or wrong entry of information.

* Do not forget to update/save the application every time there is a change made to each field and page.

2. Prepare, scan (or take a screenshot) then upload all the documentary requirements in the online admission system. Please take note of the following:

* The 2 x 2 ID photo (in white background) file size must be at most

* All the original documentary requirements must be signed by the authorized school personnel before scanning

* Screenshot of the documentary requirement using a cellphone is allowed

* Each documentary requirement is limited to 1mb (1024kb) in size only The file type of each documentary requirement must be either jpeg/png/bmp type Upload the scanned pages individually (per page)

DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENTS FOR FIRST YEAR APPLICANTS (CURRENT GRADE 12 STUDENTS)

* Completed grade 11 card (1st and 2nd semester)

* Certificate from the principal or adviser indicating that the applicant is currently enrolled as a grade 12 student with the track/strand and school year indicated



DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENTS FOR FIRST YEAR APPLICANTS (SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL GRADUATE)

* Completed grade 12 report card (1st and 2nd semester)

* Certificate of non-issuance of form 137/sf-10 for college admission (this certification shall prove that the applicant has never been enrolled in another university/college)
