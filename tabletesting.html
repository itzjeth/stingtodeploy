PROCEDURE FOR APPLICATION FOR COLLEGE ADMISSION
1. Access the admission link on a website browser then sign up with a Gmail account. Download and Fill out the online application form, Application Form keep in mind the following:

* The declared track/strand in the online application form and the track/strand indicated in the documentary requirement should match.

* Choose the correct entry type/category of applicant.

* Thoroughly check all information input in the online application form before saving and submitting to avoid typographical errors, wrong spelling, incorrect entries, or wrong entry of information.

* Do not forget to update/save the application every time there is a change made to each field and page.

2. Prepare, scan (or take a screenshot) then upload all the documentary requirements in the online admission system. Please take note of the following:

* The 2 x 2 ID photo (in white background) file size must be at most

* All the original documentary requirements must be signed by the authorized school personnel before scanning

* Screenshot of the documentary requirement using a cellphone is allowed

* Each documentary requirement is limited to 1mb (1024kb) in size only The file type of each documentary requirement must be either jpeg/png/bmp type Upload the scanned pages individually (per page)

DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENTS FOR FIRST YEAR APPLICANTS (CURRENT GRADE 12 STUDENTS)

* Completed grade 11 card (1st and 2nd semester)

* Certificate from the principal or adviser indicating that the applicant is currently enrolled as a grade 12 student with the track/strand and school year indicated



DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENTS FOR FIRST YEAR APPLICANTS (SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL GRADUATE)

* Completed grade 12 report card (1st and 2nd semester)

* Certificate of non-issuance of form 137/sf-10 for college admission (this certification shall prove that the applicant has never been enrolled in another university/college)
DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENT FOR FIRST YEAR APPLICANTS (ALS COMPLETER/PASSER)

* Certificate of Rating (COR) with eligibility to enroll in college

DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENT FOR ASSOCIATE, CERTIFICATE, VOCATIONAL, OR DIPLOMA DEGREE HOLDER

* Transcript of Records (TOR) with graduation date

DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENT FOR TRANSFEREE APPLICANTS

* Certificate of Grades (COG) or Transcript of Records (TOR) - all enrolled subjects must have final grades

DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENT FOR SECOND-COURSE APPLICANTS

* Transcript of Records (TOR) with graduation date

DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENT FOR TCP APPLICANTS

* Transcript of Records (TOR) with graduation date

3. Applicant will select the next available date and time for the validation of the application and save the schedule from the online admission system. It is important to save the schedule.

NOTE: Applicant MUST select the date and time of validation. Once the validation appointment has been saved, the information details cannot be edited again.

On-site validation is from October 20, 2025 to February 19, 2026.

4. On the appointment date, the applicant will present the original copies of documentary requirements at the Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS).

NOTE: Only applicants who are scheduled on the particular date and time will be accommodated. Strictly no-walk ins.

5. Once the application has been validated by the OSAS personnel, the applicant will be scheduled to take the admission examination (on-site).

The applicant MUST download and print the exam permit from the online admission system (after validation) and bring the exam permit on the examination day.

6. Transferee applicants, second course takers, and associate/certificate/ vocational/diploma degree holders will only undergo department evaluation and will not take the admission exam.

The result of the evaluation from the department shall determine if the applicant is qualified to proceed with the application or not.

7. TCP applicants will only undergo college evaluation from the Teacher Education Department (TED) and will not take the admission exam.





REMINDERS FOR APPLICANTS DURING EXAMINATION
* Applicants must print their Exam Permit on a letter-sized (short) bond paper and bring it on the examination day.

* Applicants with NO physical copy of the exam permit SHALL NOT be allowed to take the exam.

* Applicants will attach a 1x1 ID picture to their examination permit. They must also bring a valid ID (School ID or government-issued ID) for verification.

* Applicants must bring two pieces of Pencil No. 2.

* Only applicants who are scheduled for the admission exam shall be allowed to enter the University premises and will be directed to the designated examination area. They will not be allowed to roam around in the campus.

* Food is not allowed inside the venue or during the examination.

* The use of cellphones, calculators, and other electronic devices during the exam is strictly prohibited and shall cause disqualification.

* Applicants must dress appropriately. Attire must not be too revealing or scandalous.

* Tampering, falsification, and editing of this exam permit will mean disqualification from taking the exam.

* Applicants are expected to come to their assigned examination schedule on time.

* Physical distancing must be observed for the entire duration of the applicants' stay in the University.

NOTES: * The result of the admission examination will be announced via our Facebook page once out, so stay posted.

Course Key Information / Admission Notes
Computer Science (BSCS) Considered a technical/IT course. SHS applicants should have 85 or above in Math, Science, and English for eligibility. Your SHS strand must align with the course requirements (e.g., STEM or TVL-ICT). Slots may be limited due to high demand, so meeting grade/strand requirements does not guarantee admission.
Information Technology (BSIT) Treated similarly to Computer Science. Applicants should come from appropriate SHS strands (usually STEM or TVL-ICT). IT/CS courses are often quota-based, so high demand may limit available slots even if requirements are met.
Business Administration / Business-related (e.g., Business Management) Offered at many campuses. There is no fixed “85% Math/Science/English” requirement; SHS report cards are generally evaluated as submitted. Admission may be more flexible than technical courses.
Education (BSEd / BEEd) Historically, a final grade of 85 or above in relevant SHS subjects is required. Your SHS strand must align with campus requirements (e.g., GAS, HUMSS, STEM depending on the campus).
Psychology (BS Psychology) Offered in many campuses. There is no universal “85 in Math/Science/English” requirement. Admission typically requires submitting your SHS report card and may include an entrance exam and/or interview.
Criminology (BS Criminology) Offered at certain campuses, including Bacoor. Freshmen must submit their HS report card (Form 138). Admission may include an entrance exam, interview, and/or additional screening as part of evaluation.
